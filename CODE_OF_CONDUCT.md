# Code Of Conduct

AI-Worker is intended to be a practical, respectful, and useful open-source project. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, releases, and related community spaces is expected to help keep that environment healthy.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when impact differs from intent.
- Keep feedback technical, specific, and actionable.
- Make room for new contributors and first-time questions.
- Credit ideas, reports, and contributions accurately.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
- Dismissing maintainers' safety, privacy, or security boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project or community.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainers at `team@aiworker.app`. Include links, screenshots, dates, and context where possible. Reports will be handled with appropriate privacy and care.
